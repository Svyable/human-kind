# Taxonomy

Machine-readable values live in `data/taxonomy.yaml`. This document explains their intended use.

## Domains

Primary domains are climate, health, poverty-and-economic-mobility, conflict-and-governance, education, information-integrity, digital-rights-and-ai-safety, biodiversity, disaster-resilience, and cross-cutting.

Assign all materially relevant domains, but avoid tag inflation.

## Maturity

- `concept` — mechanism is preliminary
- `researched` — prior work and evidence landscape are substantially mapped
- `designed` — intervention and theory of change are concrete
- `pilot-ready` — ownership, ethics, safeguards, metrics, and stop conditions are defined
- `piloted` — a bounded test has occurred
- `operational` — implementation exists beyond a pilot

## Evidence levels

- `hypothesis` — plausible claim without sufficient direct evidence
- `suggestive` — some relevant evidence, important uncertainty remains
- `supported` — multiple credible sources or strong direct evidence support the claim
- `strong` — convergent high-quality evidence with limited material contradiction
- `contested` — credible evidence materially disagrees

Evidence level describes support, not importance.

## Other fields

Time horizon, intervention type, cost band, reversibility, geography, and status are controlled vocabularies so humans and agents can query the corpus consistently.
