# Charter

## Mission

Human Kind is a version-controlled commons for finding, testing, and improving ideas that could reduce suffering and expand human flourishing through evidence, humility, participation, and measurable action.

## What this repository is

It is an open research and design commons organized around **idea dossiers**. A dossier is bounded enough to investigate, challenge, test, update, or decline.

## What it is not

It is not a popularity contest, a catalog of unsupported grand solutions, an authority that speaks for affected communities, or an autonomous agent decision system.

## Lifecycle

`Intake → Triage → Research → Design → Pilot → Evaluation → Adopt / Archive`

Movement through the lifecycle represents increasing responsibility and evidence, not moral worth.

## Commitments

- Preserve uncertainty and dissent.
- Prefer falsifiable claims and reversible steps.
- Search for prior work before claiming novelty.
- Treat affected people as participants in decisions, not merely beneficiaries.
- Record failure, negative evidence, and `not-pursuing` decisions.
- Keep consequential decisions accountable to humans.
- Avoid creating avoidable dual-use, privacy, exploitation, or displacement harms.
