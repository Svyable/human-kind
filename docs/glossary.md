# Glossary

**Affected community** — people or ecosystems that materially bear benefits, burdens, risks, or decision consequences.

**Dossier** — the versioned folder that contains an idea's metadata, proposal, evidence, risks, and update history.

**Evidence level** — a calibrated description of support for a claim or dossier, not a measure of moral importance.

**Falsifier** — evidence or observation that would materially weaken the central claim.

**Human reviewer** — an accountable person who verifies agent-assisted work and owns decisions within their authority.

**Pilot** — a bounded, ethical, measurable, and preferably reversible test designed to reduce a specific uncertainty.

**Reversibility** — how feasible it is to stop, undo, or contain an intervention and its effects.

**Theory of change** — a causal explanation connecting an intervention to expected outcomes through explicit mechanisms and assumptions.
