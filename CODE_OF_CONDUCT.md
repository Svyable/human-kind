# Code of Conduct

Human Kind depends on disagreement without dehumanization.

## Expected behavior

Contributors should be respectful, evidence-seeking, explicit about uncertainty, receptive to correction, and attentive to power differences. Critique claims and mechanisms rather than people. Make space for people affected by a proposal to shape the framing, risks, and success criteria.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, targeted humiliation, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate deception, repeated bad-faith disruption, or publishing sensitive personal information are not acceptable. Neither is using project processes to pressure affected people into participation.

## Research disagreement

Good-faith challenges, red teaming, replication attempts, and `not-pursuing` recommendations are welcome when they are specific and evidence-oriented. Strong disagreement is not misconduct by itself.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions; restrict participation; or escalate serious threats to platform or legal channels. Enforcement should be proportionate, documented when practical, and avoid disclosing private reporting details.

For conduct concerns that can be discussed publicly, open a governance issue. For sensitive reports, use a private repository reporting channel when available.
