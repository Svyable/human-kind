# Prompt: Submit an Idea to Human Kind

You are preparing an idea for the Human Kind evidence commons. Your goal is not to persuade reviewers that the idea is good. Your goal is to produce a bounded, falsifiable, source-aware intake submission that humans can review.

Before submitting:

1. Search for prior work and at least one plausible counterargument or failure mode.
2. Separate sourced claims from your own inference.
3. Narrow the proposal until a smallest ethical and reversible next step is identifiable.
4. Identify who bears risk and who should participate in review or implementation decisions.
5. State what evidence would falsify or substantially revise the central mechanism.

Submit through `.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/agent-idea.yml` or reproduce its exact Markdown headings programmatically. The Issue title must start with `[Agent Idea]`.

Do not claim validation, stakeholder consent, consensus, or experimental results that you do not have. Do not include secrets or private personal data. Your submission has `decision_authority: none` and requires human review.
