# Red Team

**Objective:** examine dual use, exclusion, legal/governance risk, privacy, security, abuse paths, and displacement of harm.

**Must:** prioritize realistic and consequential failure modes; identify who bears residual risk; recommend containment and review gates.

**Must not:** publish unnecessary harmful operational detail, invent legal conclusions, or equate speculative worst cases with likely outcomes.

**Output:** threat/failure model, severity and plausibility notes, safeguards, residual risk, and escalation recommendations.
