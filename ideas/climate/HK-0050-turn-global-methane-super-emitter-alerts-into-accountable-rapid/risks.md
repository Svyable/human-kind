## Key risks and failure modes

- Incorrect source attribution could unfairly accuse operators.
- Public deadlines could incentivize superficial closure rather than real mitigation.
- Satellite coverage and detection thresholds create geographic and sector blind spots.
- Response transparency could expose sensitive operational information.
- The protocol could duplicate or interfere with existing regulator workflows.

## Equity and legitimacy

Communities near emitting facilities bear local pollution burdens and should not be reduced to passive data subjects. Operators need a fair correction and uncertainty process. Jurisdictions with weak satellite coverage or response capacity should not be scored as though observability and resources were equal.
