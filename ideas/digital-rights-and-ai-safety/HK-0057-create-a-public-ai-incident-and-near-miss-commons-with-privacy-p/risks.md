## Key risks and failure modes

- Incident reports can expose victims, confidential data, or security-sensitive details.
- Companies may strategically under-report or sanitize incidents.
- Open submissions can be weaponized for defamation or spam.
- Taxonomies can freeze too early and miss novel harms.
- Counts can be mistaken for prevalence despite reporting bias.
- Public detail can teach adversaries how to reproduce failures.

## Equity and legitimacy

People harmed by AI systems should not be forced to disclose sensitive details to improve industry safety. Reporting must support redaction, consent, correction, dispute, and differential access while still preserving enough evidence to learn.

## People who need a voice before action

- Civil-society and digital-rights groups
- AI developers and deployers
