## Key risks and failure modes

- Tests may overfit agents to known examples instead of improving general quality.
- A narrow benchmark may create false confidence while missing novel failure modes.
- Evaluation criteria may encode maintainer assumptions without sufficient affected-community input.
- The corpus could accidentally preserve sensitive or harmful operational details if examples are not curated carefully.

## Equity and legitimacy

Evaluation cases should not treat maintainers as the only source of acceptable judgment. Sensitive-domain examples should include review criteria informed by relevant experts and, where feasible, people affected by the class of intervention being modeled. The corpus should avoid representing contested value judgments as objective correctness.

## People who need a voice before action

- Repository maintainers
- Agent workflow maintainers
- Domain reviewers for sensitive test cases
- Affected-community reviewers when evaluation criteria encode material distributional or legitimacy judgments
