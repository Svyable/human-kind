## Key risks and failure modes

- Forecast false positives can spend scarce funds before events that do not materialize.
- False negatives can exclude people who are harmed.
- Recipient lists may be stale or politically captured.
- Digital payments can fail during disasters or exclude people without accounts or connectivity.
- Cash can be ineffective where markets fail.
- Rigid triggers may ignore local knowledge and compound hazards.

## Equity and legitimacy

Trigger and targeting design must include people living in exposed areas, especially those without formal IDs, bank accounts, stable housing, or historical administrative visibility. False-negative harm must be measured, not hidden behind average forecast accuracy.
