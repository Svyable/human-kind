# Risks

## Failure modes

What could fail technically, socially, economically, politically, or operationally?

## Misuse and dual use

How could the intervention, data, tooling, or published detail be abused?

## Distributional effects

Who may benefit, who may bear costs, and who may be excluded or displaced?

## Reversibility and containment

What can be stopped or undone? What effects may persist?

## Safeguards and stop conditions

What controls are required before testing, and what observations should pause or terminate work?

## Residual uncertainty

Which risks remain unresolved after safeguards?
