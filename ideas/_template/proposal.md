# Proposal

## Problem

What exact problem exists, for whom, in which context, and at what scale?

## Existing work

What solutions, institutions, policies, products, community practices, and research already address it?

## Proposed intervention

What specifically would change? What is novel here, if anything?

## Causal mechanism

Why should the intervention cause the desired outcome? List critical assumptions.

## Falsifiers

What evidence or observation would materially weaken the central claim?

## Smallest responsible next step

What is the smallest ethical, reversible experiment or research action that would reduce a key uncertainty?

## Participation and ownership

Who must be involved, especially affected communities, domain experts, implementers, and accountable owners?
