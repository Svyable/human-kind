## Key risks and failure modes

- Even minimal metadata can expose sources through inference.
- Metrics can pressure responders into unsafe or escalatory action.
- Armed actors could exploit knowledge of alert mechanisms.
- Communities may be promised response capacity that does not exist.
- Central systems may erase local context and informal warning channels.
- False or manipulated alerts can cause harm.

## Equity and legitimacy

Community members are not sensors for external institutions. They need control over what is recorded, realistic expectations about response, safe opt-out, and meaningful participation in defining success and acceptable risk.

## People who need a voice before action

- Community liaison and protection practitioners
- Conflict-affected community representatives
- Civilian-protection experts
- Human-rights and humanitarian-law experts
- Security and privacy specialists
- Gender and protection advisers
- Local peacebuilding organizations
