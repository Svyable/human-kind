## Key risks and failure modes

- Environmental detection may not map cleanly to human clinical risk.
- Sampling can stigmatize communities, farms, hospitals, or industries.
- Methods and targets may not be comparable across laboratories.
- High-resource settings may dominate global coverage.
- Signals could trigger inappropriate antibiotic or infection-control responses.
- Data could be misused for punitive enforcement without causal evidence.

## Equity and legitimacy

Sentinel placement must not turn marginalized communities into disproportionately monitored populations. Countries and communities generating samples should share governance and benefit from resulting capacity and knowledge.
