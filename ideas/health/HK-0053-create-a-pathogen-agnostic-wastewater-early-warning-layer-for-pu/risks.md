## Key risks and failure modes

- Wastewater signals can be misinterpreted as precise case counts.
- Sewer coverage is unequal and absent in many communities.
- Sampling sites can create privacy or stigmatization risks at small geographic scales.
- Multiplexing may trade breadth for analytical sensitivity.
- False alarms could cause costly or coercive responses.
- Sustained laboratory and data capacity may be expensive.

## Equity and legitimacy

The system must not make sewer-connected populations the default model of humanity. Non-sewered communities require alternative environmental surveillance approaches, and small-area reporting needs safeguards against stigmatization and re-identification.
