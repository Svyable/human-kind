## Key risks and failure modes

- Credential absence may be misread as evidence of fakery.
- Trusted signers can publish misleading content with valid provenance.
- Identity assertions can endanger anonymous or vulnerable creators.
- Metadata can be stripped in common workflows.
- Centralized trust lists can privilege powerful institutions.
- UI simplification can erase uncertainty and nuance.

## Equity and legitimacy

Anonymous journalists, activists, survivors, and creators using older or low-cost tools must not become second-class speakers. The system should preserve pseudonymity and clearly communicate that provenance verifies history/signatures, not factual truth.
